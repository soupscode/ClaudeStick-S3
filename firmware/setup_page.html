first-time setup
wi-fi
Your Stick needs your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz).
time zone & clock
For the clock on the Stick. It sets itself over the internet; if your network has none, this page sets it for you.
you & your stick
Always on: prompts wait for the Stick while it is connected, and go back to the terminal when it isn't.
wi-fi networks
The Stick joins the strongest one it can see. Add your phone's hotspot (2.4 GHz, WPA2) to use it away from home.
remote access (bridge)
Run the ClaudeStick bridge on a computer at home and the Stick follows your Claude sessions from any Wi-Fi: approve, talk to Claude, stop it. See docs/REMOTE.md.

remote mode
Away from your desk with the Stick? Choose always on: prompts wait for the Stick (or phone) while it is connected, and go back to the terminal as soon as it isn't.




pair a device
Set up the Stick, your phone or another computer with a one-time 6-digit code instead of the long token. It works once, for 5 minutes, and dies after 3 wrong tries.




public address
How your phone and the Stick reach this bridge from outside: your reverse proxy's address. Used for the phone link and the buttons in ntfy alerts.





macros
One per line: label | instruction. Shown on the Stick, the phone page and the Done menu. Max 10.





approvals & done menu













voice (push-to-talk)
Any OpenAI-compatible speech-to-text server, e.g. speaches / faster-whisper on your network: http://your-server:8000/v1/audio/transcriptions. Hold A on the Stick to talk.








phone alerts (optional)
Push via ntfy (self-host it or use ntfy.sh) in remote mode, with Approve / Always / Deny buttons that work when your phone can reach this device (home Wi-Fi, Tailscale, or a bridge's public address).
